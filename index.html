ElectroFusion

The fusion between research and curiosity.

ElectroFusion is the student led research society for physics and electrical, electronics engineering at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology. We built this space for the ideas that don't fit neatly into a syllabus. We’re a community of students who learn by doing — because we'd much rather burn our fingers on a soldering iron than read another chapter.

Domains

Six disciplines, one board

Power & Energy Systems

Power electronics, smart grids, and renewable energy integration.

Control Systems & Automation

Control theory, motor control, drive systems, automation, and intelligent control.

Electronics & Embedded Systems

Embedded systems, digital/analog electronics, microcontrollers, sensors, and electronic hardware.

VLSI & Chip Design

Digital and analog IC design, FPGAs, and semiconductor fundamentals.

Analog & Mixed-Signal Electronics

Analog circuit design, current-mode circuits, amplifiers, filters, oscillators, and signal-processing blocks.

Signal Processing & Emerging Electroics

Analog signal processing, memristive circuits, energy-efficient architectures, and emerging computing technologies.
