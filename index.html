ElectroFusion

Where circuits, code, and current converge.

ElectroFusion is the student society for electrical, electronics, instrumentation, and computer engineering at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology. We're a lab bench for the ideas that don't fit in a syllabus — built by students who'd rather solder something than just read about it.

Join the Society Explore What We Do

01 — About

A society built like the systems we design

Electrical and electronics engineering rarely stays inside one discipline — a single project can pull in power systems, firmware, signal processing, and PCB layout all at once. ElectroFusion exists so students don't have to chase those skills alone. It's where coursework turns into hardware, and where first-years and final-years end up debugging the same circuit at 11pm.

Whether you're picking up a soldering iron for the first time or already have a stack of finished boards, there's a bench here for you.

02 — Domains

Six disciplines, one board

Every ElectroFusion project draws from at least one of these — most draw from three or four.

Power & Energy Systems

Power electronics, smart grids, and renewable energy integration.

Embedded Systems & IoT

Microcontrollers, firmware, sensor networks, and connected devices.

Robotics & Automation

Control systems, mechatronics, and autonomous machines.

VLSI & Chip Design

Digital and analog IC design, FPGAs, and semiconductor fundamentals.

Signal Processing & Comms

DSP, wireless communication, and RF system design.

Circuit Design & PCB Engineering

Analog/digital circuit design, PCB layout, and hardware prototyping.

03 — Workflow

How we build

Every project — from a weekend build to a semester-long one — moves through the same five stages.

  1. 01

    Ideate

    Spot a problem worth solving and sketch the concept.

  2. 02

    Design

    Simulate circuits, plan the architecture, choose components.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    Breadboard it, order the PCB, write the firmware.

  4. 04

    Test & Iterate

    Debug, measure, refine — repeat until it's solid.

  5. 05

    Showcase

    Present at expos, workshops, and competitions.
