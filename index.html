ElectroFusion

Where circuits, code, and current converge.

ElectroFusion is the student society for electrical, electronics, instrumentation, and computer engineering at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology. We're a lab bench for the ideas that don't fit in a syllabus — built by students who'd rather solder something than just read about it.

Domains

Six disciplines, one board

Every ElectroFusion project draws from at least one of these — most draw from three or four.

Power & Energy Systems

Power electronics, smart grids, and renewable energy integration.

Embedded Systems & IoT

Microcontrollers, firmware, sensor networks, and connected devices.

Robotics & Automation

Control systems, mechatronics, and autonomous machines.

VLSI & Chip Design

Digital and analog IC design, FPGAs, and semiconductor fundamentals.

Signal Processing & Comms

DSP, wireless communication, and RF system design.

Circuit Design & PCB Engineering

Analog/digital circuit design, PCB layout, and hardware prototyping.
