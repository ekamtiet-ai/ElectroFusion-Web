01 — About

A society built like the systems we design

Electrical and electronics engineering rarely stays inside one discipline — a single project can pull in power systems, firmware, signal processing, and PCB layout all at once. ElectroFusion exists so students don't have to chase those skills alone. It's where coursework turns into hardware, and where first-years and final-years end up debugging the same circuit at 11pm.

Whether you're picking up a soldering iron for the first time or already have a stack of finished boards, there's a bench here for you.

03 — Workflow

How we build

Every project — from a weekend build to a semester-long one — moves through the same five stages.

  1. 01

    Ideate

    Spot a problem worth solving and sketch the concept.

  2. 02

    Design

    Simulate circuits, plan the architecture, choose components.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    Breadboard it, order the PCB, write the firmware.

  4. 04

    Test & Iterate

    Debug, measure, refine — repeat until it's solid.

  5. 05

    Showcase

    Present at expos, workshops, and competitions.
