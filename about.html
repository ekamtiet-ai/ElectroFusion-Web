01 — About

A society built like the systems we design

Electrical and electronics engineering rarely stays inside one discipline — a single project can pull in power systems, firmware, signal processing, and PCB layout all at once.

ElectroFusion exists so students don't have to chase those skills alone. It's where coursework turns into hardware, and where first-years and final-years end up debugging the same circuit at 11pm.

Whether you're picking up a soldering iron for the first time or already have a stack of finished boards, there's a bench here for you.
