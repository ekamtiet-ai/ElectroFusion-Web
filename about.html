Workflow

How we build

Every project — from a weekend build to a semester-long one — moves through the same six stages.

  1. 01

    Ideate

    Spot a problem worth solving and sketch the concept.

  2. 02

    Design

    Plan the architecture, draft schematics, and choose components.

  3. 03

    Simulate

    Run virtual tests, verify logic, and catch errors before building.

  4. 04

    Prototype

    Breadboard it, order the PCB, write the firmware.

  5. 05

    Test & Iterate

    Debug, measure, refine — repeat until it's solid.

  6. 06

    Showcase

    Present at expos, workshops, and competitions.
